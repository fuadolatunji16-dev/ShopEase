What's ShopEase?

ShopEase simplifies organizing shopping lists with real-time sharing, inventory tracking, and receipt attachments.
"A smart solution to everyday shopping, seamlessly integrated into one app!"
Top Features of Our App
Track home inventory
Keep an organized record of your household items and monitor stock levels with ease.
Easily create shopping list
Quickly add and manage your shopping items with images for an efficient trip.
Scan your receipts easily
Capture and store your receipts with just a snap for easy reference.
Collaborate with friends/family
Share and manage shopping lists with your friends/family in real-time.
Get started with ShopEase
  • Step 1: Sign up with your mobile number
  • - Download & install the app from Play Store or App Store.
  • - Provide your mobile number.
  • - Enter the OTP to successfully sign in to the app.
  • Step 2: Create the shopping list
  • - Navigate to the checklist.
  • - Add items using the scan option or the add option.
  • - Link the items to a specific shop.
  • - Check off the items as you purchase them.
  • - Complete your shopping.
  • Step 3: Upload the receipt
  • - Upload the receipt when the purchase is completed or do it later from the history.
  • - Click on the receipt image placeholder.
  • - Upload from the gallery or take a picture.
  • - Successfully attach the invoice.
All items are updated to your inventory list, ensuring your stock is replenished after completing the purchase.
